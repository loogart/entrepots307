Units and Access

011. How can storage units properly preserve my belongings?

Our warehouses, built entirely of concrete, are among the only ones in the Outaouais region offering resistance to fire, weather, mold, and insects. They feature thermal insulation to maintain stable temperatures, interior and exterior lighting, and camera surveillance

022. How can I rent a storage unit with Entrepôts 307?

You can reserve your unit online, choose the available size and unit, then proceed to payment by credit card. Once payment is made, you'll receive your contract by email, along with our regulations.

033. How can I take possession of my unit?

As soon as you receive your contract, you can go directly to your unit and access it whenever you like.

044. How can I pay my bill?

Payment is made online by credit card (Visa, Mastercard) at the time of booking.

055. What is the minimum rental period?

All our leases are monthly (28 days) and automatically renewable until cancelled, with 15 days notice.

066. Do you offer long-term leases?

Yes, we offer short-term rentals (renewal every 28 days with 15 days’ notice for cancellations) and long-term rentals (1-year leases for our commercial garages).

077. How can I cancel my lease?

Just give us a 15-day notice through your customer account before the end of the rental cycle. Once your items are picked up, please remember to remove your lock.

088. Are my belongings insured?

No, it's your responsibility to insure your belongings. Most homeowner's and renter's insurance companies cover self-storage units. Inform your insurer of your use of a self-storage unit.

099. What are the opening hours for office staff?

We're open Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.

101. When can I access my unit?

You can access your property at any time, 24/7.

112. Can I drive to my unit?

Yes, our facilities allow vehicle access.

123. Will my unit be heated?

Our concrete warehouses offer excellent protection against humidity, making heating unnecessary for most goods. However, we also offer heated mini-warehouses to meet your specific needs.

134. Can I store vehicles?

Yes, our warehouses are made entirely of concrete, providing a safe place to store your vehicles all year round.

145. Do I need a padlock?

Yes, our units do not include padlocks. You must use your own padlock. Almost all models are accepted.

156. What items are not allowed in my storage unit?

It is forbidden to store dangerous or illegal items, such as flammable substances, weapons, drugs, perishable foodstuffs, etc.

167. How can I best organize the contents of my storage unit?

We recommend stacking heavy boxes under lighter ones, storing pictures and mirrors on the sides, and placing mattresses on the bottom. Leave a passageway between boxes for easy access and space for good ventilation. Also, clear space near heating sources (e.g., electric baseboard heaters) to ensure the safety and efficiency of your storage.
