FAQ

Contracts and Administration

How can storage units properly preserve my belongings?

Our warehouses are among the only ones in the Outaouais built entirely of concrete, offering resistance to fire, weather, mold and insects. Their thermal insulation maintains stable temperatures, and they include interior and exterior lighting and camera surveillance.

How can I rent a storage unit with Entrepôts 307?

You can reserve your unit online, choose the size and available unit, then pay by credit card. Once payment is complete, you will receive an access code by email along with your contract and regulations.

How do I take possession of my unit?

An access code will be emailed to you after payment so you can enter the secure storage area.

How can I pay my invoice?

Payment is made online by credit card (Visa, Mastercard) when you reserve.

What is the minimum rental period?

All leases are monthly (28 days) and renew automatically until cancelled, with 15 days' notice.

Do you offer long-term rental contracts?

Yes, we offer short-term rentals and long-term options, including 1-year leases for our commercial garages.

How can I cancel my rental contract?

Simply give us 15 days' notice through your customer account before the end of the rental cycle. Once your belongings are removed, you can remove your padlock.

Are my belongings insured?

No, it is your responsibility to insure your belongings. Most homeowner or tenant insurance companies cover self-storage units. Notify your insurer that you are using a storage unit.

What are the office hours?

We are open Monday to Friday, from 8:30 a.m. to 4 p.m.

When can I access my unit?

You can access your belongings at any time, 24/7, with your secure access code.

Can I drive up to my unit?

Yes, our facilities allow vehicle access.

Will my unit be heated?

Our concrete warehouses provide excellent protection against humidity, making heating unnecessary for most belongings. However, we also offer heated mini-storage units for specific needs.

Can I store vehicles?

Yes, our warehouses are built entirely of concrete, offering a safe space to store your vehicles year-round.

Do I need a padlock?

Yes, our units do not include a padlock. You must use your own padlock. Almost all models are accepted.

What items are prohibited in my storage unit?

It is prohibited to store dangerous or illegal items, such as flammable substances, weapons, drugs, perishable food, etc.

How can I organize my storage unit efficiently?

Stack heavy boxes under lighter boxes, store paintings and mirrors on their sides, and place mattresses at the back. Leave a passage between boxes for easy access and ventilation.
