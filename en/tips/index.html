Tips

Here are a few practical tips to ensure the safety and integrity of your goods during storage.

Before Storing

  • Deep Cleaning: Thoroughly clean your appliances before storing them. Keep doors partially open to prevent mold growth.
  • Rust Prevention: Apply a small amount of machine oil to bikes, tools, and other metal equipment to prevent rust.
  • Labelling: Clearly label your boxes, listing all items they contain, especially fragile items.

Organizing Your Unit

  • Elevate Items: Use wooden pallets or 2×4s to keep items off the floor.
  • Freestanding Shelving: Install freestanding shelving for better organization, visibility, and accessibility.
  • Vertical Storage: Optimize space by stacking chairs and storing mattresses and box springs lengthwise.
  • Balanced Weight: Ensure boxes are balanced in weight to prevent tipping or breaking.

Protecting Your Goods

  • Regular Check-Ups: Get into the habit of inspecting your storage unit once a month to ensure everything is in order.
  • Insurance: Take out appropriate insurance to cover any potential losses.
  • Protective Covers: Use protective covers to shield mattresses and furniture from dust and scratches.
  • Careful Packaging: Pack electronic devices in appropriate boxes with cushioning materials to prevent damage.
  • Furniture Protection: Oil and wrap your furniture properly to avoid scratches and damage.

Inventory and Organization

  • Detailed Inventory: Create a detailed inventory of each box, listing its contents and assigning a number for easy reference.
  • Effective Sealing: Use quality adhesive tape to seal your boxes securely, ensuring their integrity during transport and storage.
  • Space Utilization: Maximize space by using boxes of different sizes, placing them in a stable and accessible position.

Avoid

  • Plastic Packaging: Avoid using plastic to cover your goods, as it can retain moisture and cause damage.
  • Food and Toxic Products: Do not store any food, toxic or illegal products in your storage unit.
  • Access Code Disclosure: Never share your access code with anyone unless you have given express permission.
